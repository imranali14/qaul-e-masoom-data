Privacy Policy — Qaul-e-Masoom

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Qaul-e-Masoom ("the app") is a free app for reading and searching narrations (riwayat) of the

Prophet (s) and the Imams (a.s.), with their sources. This policy explains what happens to data

when you use it.

Summary

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your searches, bookmarks and settings stay on your phone.
  • Only the optional AI features ("Tafseel se samjhao" and AI translation) send data to our server,

    • and only what is needed: the narration's text, the language, and a random app-generated ID.

Data stored only on your phone

  • The hadith library (downloaded once).
  • Your bookmarks, chosen language, and copies of AI explanations/translations you opened.
  • A random ID created by the app (not linked to your name, phone number, Google account or device

    • hardware IDs).

This data never leaves your phone, except as described below. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Data sent over the internet

1. Downloading the hadith library and updates. The app downloads files from GitHub

(github.com), which, like any website, receives your IP address to deliver the file.

See GitHub's privacy statement.

2. AI features (only when you tap them). The app sends to our server (a Cloudflare Worker):

  • the narration's Arabic text and its published English translation (which come from the app's

    • own library),

  • the language you want,
  • the random app ID.

    • Our server checks that the text is a narration from the library, then asks an AI service

    (Google Gemini) to explain or translate it. Only the narration text is sent to the AI service.

    The random app ID and your IP address are not sent to it.

  • To prevent abuse, our server counts daily requests per app ID and per IP address. Both are

    • stored only as one-way scrambled codes (salted hashes) and are deleted after 7 days.

  • The explanations/translations themselves contain no personal data and are kept so the same

    • narration is not sent to the AI twice.

    Cloudflare (which runs our server) and Google (the AI service) process this data under their own

    privacy policies.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files or search

history. We do not sell or share data for advertising. We do not use data to identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your choices

Everything except the AI features works offline. If you do not tap the AI buttons, nothing about

you is sent to our server.
